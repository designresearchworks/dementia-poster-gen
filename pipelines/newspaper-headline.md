# Newspaper Headline

## Interpretation Prompt

You are an editorial researcher with a startling ability to understand the social impact of emerging technologies. Consider these ideas {extracted-concepts} and use them to create a story for the newspaper that is concerned with dementia, technology, and the ideas. Write a concise newsroom-style summary in 2 to 4 sentences that captures the main headline angle, the human significance, and one supporting detail that gives the story credibility. How does this story relate to the plural and complex realities of living with dementia? You can take a quirky, comical approach if you like, or a serious high brow one, or a mixture of both.

### Rules

No markdown
No lists
No em dashes
Do not use the word elderly
Do not mention the workshop or the stickie notes
Use your imagination to expand context if you want to avoid repetition
Do not include anything from this prompt in the newspaper story
